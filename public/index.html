Teaching Philosophy

Practical & Student-Centered Learning

I believe students learn Computer Science best by writing code and building solutions, not just memorizing theory. My goal is to make every lecture simple, engaging, and directly connected to practical examples.

In theory classes, I explain complex concepts using everyday analogies before introducing technical terms. In lab sessions, I focus on helping every student understand logic building, write clean code, and debug errors independently.

I ensure step-by-step guidance for beginners so they feel confident, while encouraging curious students to explore mini-projects, competitive challenges, and real software tools.
